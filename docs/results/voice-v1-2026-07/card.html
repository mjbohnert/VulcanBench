Grok Voice Think Fast 2.0 vs. GPT Realtime (the voice tax, measured)

Two hundred held-out questions, asked twice: typed, then spoken via TTS. One scorer for both modes.
Speaking the question costs both models accuracy, but not much. Grok leads on text, on audio, and pays the smaller tax. Spoken arithmetic drives the loss for both.
