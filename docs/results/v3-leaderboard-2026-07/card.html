The v3 Leaderboard (every model we’ve measured)

Twenty-three real merged PRs across Python, Rust, TypeScript, JavaScript, and Go, graded by deterministic hidden tests
Grok 4.5 (medium) owns the suite: 21/23 at $0.32 per solved task — nothing else matches it on either axis. Claude Opus 5 debuts level with Fable 5’s best at 20/23, and is the only model whose score falls at every step up the effort knob.
